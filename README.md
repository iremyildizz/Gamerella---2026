# You're Next

A tense, first-person zombie survival game originally made in 2 days for **GAMERella Jam 2026**, with development continuing post-jam.

Your objective is to hack the main datacenter to save humanity while defending your bunker from incoming zombies. Manage your survival needs, maintain your surveillance and defense systems, and complete the hack before your defenses fail.

## Gameplay & Mechanics

* **Main Objective — Datacenter Hacking:** Interact with your computer terminal to hack the main datacenter and win the game.
* **Bunker Defense & Zapping:** Keep an eye on the 4-camera security feed (`cam 01` – `cam 04`), replace camera batteries before they shut off, activate switches to zap zombies, and reinforce your windows and door so your protection meter doesn't hit zero.
* **Survival & Needs Management:** Balance your **Hunger**, **Sleep**, and **Protection** levels. Eat food to avoid starving, and sleep to replenish your sleep meter—just be careful, as sleeping increases hunger and leaves your bunker undefended while you're asleep.

## Controls

| Action | Input |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| **Look** | `Mouse` |
| **Interact / Hack / Repair / Eat** | `Point & Left Click` |
| **Toggle Needs UI** | `Q` |

## Tech Stack

* **Engine:** Unity (C#)
* **Input:** Unity Input System
* **UI & Visuals:** TextMeshPro, Custom Post-Processing Shaders

## Installation & Play

1. Download `You're next.zip` (Windows build) from the [itch.io page](https://pablocabale.itch.io/you-re-next).
2. Extract the archive and run the executable.
